![weekly kite folding invite](/assets/img/artist-residency-pnj.jpg)

<p>I am very enthusiastic to announce this collaborative between the <a href='https://www.abqpeaceandjustice.org/home'>Albuquerque Peace and Justice Center</a> and <a href='https://bakesy.shop/b/noor-sweets-eats'>Noor's Sweets and Eats</a>. We are hosting weekly kite folding sessions at the Peace and Justice Center <strong>Tuesdays and Fridays 10am-noon</strong>. If there is enough interest, we may open other weekly time slots in the future. During some of the sessions, we will have delectable treats catered by Noor's Sweets and Eats who shares their profits with people in need.</p>

<p>Here are some of the goals for this residency:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Designing portable displays for the kites to be exhibited at more locations in Albuquerque and elsewhere.</li>
  <li>Inviting more people to fold kites together and help bring this project to completion.</li>
  <li>Practicing our collective efforts for Palestine and collective liberation while being in community.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>The Albuquerque Peace and Justice Center is located at 202 Harvard SE, Albuquerque NM 87106</strong>. We hope to meet you at these events. We also continue to book kite folding sessions for organizations, community events, and private homes. Contact <a href='mailto:info@abqkitesforgaza.art'>info@abqkitesforgaza.art</a> to schedule an event for your organization, school, faith-based institution, or for your friends and family members. Visit the <a href='/events'>events page</a> for upcoming public events in the community</p>

<p>We continue to work with Among the Rubble Collective to help them raise funds for families in Gaza. Among the Rubble is currently trying to evacuate a family of 12 while the border crossing is open. <strong>We are at 73% of the goal. Help us reach 100%!</strong></p>
