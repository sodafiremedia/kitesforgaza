<p>The Community Kite Project for Gaza is traveling to Santa Fe, NM! We are partnering with <a href='https://www.instagram.com/swc4palestine/'>Southwest Coalition for Palestine</a>, <a href='https://www.instagram.com/palestine_solidarity_santa_fe'>Palestine Solidarity Santa Fe</a>, and <a href='https://www.santafeansforjusticeinpalestine.org'>Santa Feans Justice for Palestine</a>, with an installation and kite folding event at <a href='https://www.elmuseocultural.org'>El Museo Cultural de Santa Fe</a>. As always, this is a family-friendly event</p>

<p>We aim to make over 1,000 kites during this event.
